# Project 2 (Group)

Dr Hady W. LAUW _ posted on May 11, 2026 8:19 PM

The purpose of the group project is to get you hands-on experience on building a recommender system based on some real-world data.

For each of the milestone below, upload the slides to eLearn/Assignments before class time in the corresponding weeks.

## June 3, 2026: Project Proposal

Present your project proposal (max. 10 minutes) to the class. It should describe:

- The dataset you would be collecting, the methodology of collection, the expected data size
- The recommendation problem you seek to solve, and why it is important or significant
- The recommender algorithm(s) you seek to implement or apply (you may use existing libraries but must clearly acknowledge sources)
- The experiments you would be running to test your models
- Any other X-factor

## June 24, 2026: Project Solution

Each group takes turn to present their project solution in the allotted time slot:

- Finalized dataset, problem, algorithms, experimental results
- Discussions on the applicability as well as significance of the project
- Proposed or attempted extensions with evidence
- Demonstration of a working recommender system
- Future work
